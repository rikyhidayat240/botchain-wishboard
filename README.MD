## 📖 What is Web3 Wish Board?

Web3 Wish Board is a decentralized application (dApp) built on the BOT Chain. It allows anyone to write a wish, hope, or message and store it permanently on the blockchain. 

Instead of writing a message on a physical wall that might fade or be painted over, your words are securely minted on-chain. This means your wish will live on the internet eternally, visible to everyone, and can never be altered or deleted.

## 🚀 Features

- **Permanent Storage:** Every message is permanently and securely recorded on the BOT Chain.
- **True Dark Mode UI:** A beautiful, modern glassmorphism design with a sleek black theme and subtle ambient animations.
- **Custom Notifications:** Get real-time, elegant alerts for all your blockchain interactions—no annoying browser pop-ups.
- **Fully Responsive:** Works perfectly and scales fluidly across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- **Real-Time Updates:** Seamlessly fetches and displays the latest wishes directly from the blockchain.

## 🛠️ How to Use It (Step-by-Step Guide)

Using the Web3 Wish Board is incredibly simple. Just follow these steps:

1. **Get a Wallet:** Make sure you have the [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/) browser extension installed and set up.
2. **Switch Network:** Ensure your MetaMask is connected to the **BOT Chain** network. 
3. **Connect Your Wallet:** Click the **"Connect Wallet"** button on the page. MetaMask will ask for your permission to connect to the site.
4. **Write Your Wish:** Type your message, dream, or hope into the text box (up to 280 characters).
5. **Send to the Blockchain:** Click the **"Send Wish"** button. This requires a tiny network fee of **0.001 BOT**.
6. **Confirm the Transaction:** MetaMask will pop up asking you to confirm the transaction. Click confirm, wait a few seconds for the network to process it, and your wish will appear on the board forever!
